Untitled (usb ports)

2022.02.14

go back


usb ports and ethernet cords carved into my tissue
coolant staying damp on my neck

the skies, the power lines drive their blades into my veins
blood staying dry on my wrist

a breath, a pulse
air circulating in flesh

remains ever still like
quivering lips
