Untitled (i'm taut on her floor.)

2026.02.10

go back

i’m taut on her floor. My body
unbosomed. her hair pouring on my
wrists. you teach me to hate. my
Transgression for viscera. each strand
a saline, licking my wounds. So I
bleed, I bleed all dreams, you
are all my tracheotomy. You
bled into me, my veins your
cruelty, your passion the
sepsis that stings. So I
dream, I dream all dreams, you
are all my neurology. You
ichor your luster, my throat your
sensuality, your skin the
gyrus that sings. So I
cease. I
