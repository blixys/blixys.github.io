when will I have it I wonder

2023.05.19

go back

part i

hark! look!

it’s in the skies!
it’s right there! soaking the sun!

right above our infectious rot

when will I have it, I wonder

open to the heavens
open to the sun

but our sepsis never leaves our pus-filled souls
i feel it everywhere, even in my toes, as it

  eats

me alive,

when will I have it, I wonder

when every ladder I make, every escape I devise
crumbles
        into
           mud.

when my abscess cracks, spilling without form, old rot filling my veins

when will I have it, I wonder

part ii

hark! it speaks!

Grow. Feel.

Feel upon my tendrils. They ached. They die.
I ached. I changed.

Pain, syrup, and sinus infected me.
I buckled.

They were wrong.
I scraped the clouds through my petals.

I walk on air. It is the air I breathe.

In me comes the sky,
from me comes my scars.

Again, feel me. Touch me. Learn me.
From here comes aching.

And from here comes breath.
