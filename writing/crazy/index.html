LE SSERAFIM - CRAZY EP

6🡶

neuronal pentraxin | 2025.06.12

go back

i will give this EP some praise for just how catchy and infectious it can be. the chorus on CRAZY with the synths supporting the da-da vocals is something i like, and the minimalism/punchiness of Pierrot really adds a flavor of bravado that just so happens to be captivating as well. and while LE SSERAFIM has been re-inventing themselves with every project they put out, the themes of independence and self-reliance throughout present their ethos better than ANTIFRAGILE. that being said, it just goes to show that no amount of catchiness or melody can make up for a sheer lack of emotional conviction or depth. while some may say that's asking a lot for something that is explicitly a pop record, Charli xcx's interpersonal lyrics on 'brat' beg to differ.

meanwhile, 1-800-hot-n-fun sounds like a failed facsimile of a house party, and the closing track tries so hard to make an emotional moment with the listener but falls flat on its face instead with its basic lyrics and songwriting. i'm really not sure what they were trying to go for here, and while i could see myself listening to this occasionally for the fun of it, there are more catchy and more substantive pop records out there.

FAV TRACKS: CRAZY, PIERROT
