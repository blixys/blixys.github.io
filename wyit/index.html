my dearest wyit, Wyatt Cole:

dated on this day where you didn't hit the bricks, march of the eleventh, twenty-twenty-six: by heavens! you're of LEGAL DRINKING AGE! oh dear! what about a legal beer for kicks? or a legal whiskey and cider for a thirsty wyatt on a quiet little schtick? what a bunch-a legal pricks to make you wait until twenty-twenty-six! but we both know you had your bag of tricks...

rhymes aside. happy birthday, and happy twenty-first to boot!

this is a genuinely difficult letter to write. not because of a lack of ideas, but because there's so many milquetoast things to say here on your twenty-first. from 'you're one of the most positive people i know!' to 'may this year bring you joy!'. we just talked in-depth about the roots of our romantic preferences -- and on such a significant occasion (it really is!) there's a mismatch there.

if we're being honest... we tend to enable each other's vices and quirks. that's gotten the both of us in trouble several times -- in some ways more severe than others. but it's not all bad! remember a time before i tweeted about sadomasochism? before you lamented about your human form? do you ever wonder if we're truer because of each other?

it seems obvious, but i can't answer that with certainty. what i can say is that it wouldn't be our friendship without it. what i can say is that we crossed each other at our most malleable, and seeing our persons start to cement... is gonna be difficult. you're twenty-one now. i'll be there before you know it.

at the same time, we've known each other through our most volatile. remember what i said about us? we've become friends several times, because we've met different versions of us at different times. i've known many wyits over many years. ditto for you. i cringe at our old messages and it's visceral proof of the most beautiful things our many friendships offer.

maybe this is the beginning of our longest. ♦
